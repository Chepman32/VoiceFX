# Privacy Policy for VoiceFX

**Last Updated: January 15, 2025**

## Introduction

VoiceFX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the VoiceFX mobile application.

## Data Collection and Storage

### What We Collect

VoiceFX is designed as an **offline-first application**. We collect minimal data:

1. **Audio Files**
   - Audio recordings you create or import
   - Stored locally on your device only
   - Never uploaded to our servers

2. **Project Data**
   - Project names, descriptions, and metadata
   - Effect settings and configurations
   - Stored locally in SQLite database
   - Never transmitted off your device

3. **User Preferences**
   - Theme settings (light/dark mode)
   - App preferences (haptics, sound effects)
   - Stored locally only

4. **Purchase Information**
   - In-App Purchase receipts
   - Managed by Apple/Google
   - We only store purchase validation locally

### What We DON'T Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Contact information (unless you email us)
- Usage analytics (unless explicitly opted in)
- Advertising identifiers
- Social media data

## How We Use Your Data

All data processing happens **locally on your device**:

1. **Audio Processing**
   - Effects are applied using device CPU
   - No cloud processing

2. **Data Storage**
   - All data stored in device storage
   - SQLite database for structured data
   - File system for audio files

3. **Exports**
   - Export files created locally
   - You control where exports are saved
   - No automatic uploads

## Data Sharing

**We do not share your data with third parties** because:
- All data stays on your device
- No network communication except IAP
- No analytics services (unless opted in)
- No advertising networks

### Third-Party Services

We use these services with minimal data:

1. **Apple App Store / Google Play Store**
   - For app distribution and updates
   - Subject to their privacy policies

2. **In-App Purchase System**
   - Apple/Google handles transactions
   - We only validate purchases locally
   - Subject to their privacy policies

3. **Optional Analytics** (requires opt-in)
   - If you enable analytics in settings
   - Basic usage statistics only
   - Anonymized and aggregated
   - Can be disabled anytime

## Data Security

We protect your data through:

1. **Local Storage**
   - Data encrypted at rest (device encryption)
   - No transmission reduces risk

2. **App Permissions**
   - Microphone: For audio recording only
   - Storage: For saving/loading projects
   - Photos: For exporting visualizations

3. **No Account Required**
   - No passwords to secure
   - No risk of account breach

## Your Rights

You have complete control:

1. **Access**: All your data is accessible in the app
2. **Delete**: Delete projects individually or all data
3. **Export**: Export your projects anytime
4. **Portability**: Data exported in standard formats
5. **Opt-out**: Disable analytics in Settings

## Data Retention

- **Project Data**: Kept until you delete it
- **Preferences**: Kept until app uninstall
- **Purchase Records**: Required for IAP validation
- **Analytics**: 90 days (if opted in)

## Children's Privacy

VoiceFX does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents can safely allow children to use the app as all data stays on device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in-app and in updates. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

## International Users

VoiceFX works offline worldwide. No data leaves your device, so international data transfer laws don't apply.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under CCPA. However, since we don't collect personal information in the traditional sense, most CCPA rights don't apply. You already have:

- Right to know: All data visible in-app
- Right to delete: Delete function in-app
- Right to opt-out: No data sold (none collected)

## European Users (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have rights regarding your data:

- **Right to access**: Access all data in-app
- **Right to rectification**: Edit projects directly
- **Right to erasure**: Delete projects/uninstall app
- **Right to data portability**: Export features
- **Right to object**: Disable analytics in Settings

Since data is local-only, you exercise these rights directly through the app.

## Data Deletion

To delete all your data:

1. **Delete Projects**: Library > Delete each project
2. **Clear Preferences**: Settings > Reset to Defaults
3. **Complete Removal**: Uninstall the app

This removes all VoiceFX data from your device.

## Technical Implementation

For transparency, here's how we ensure privacy:

1. **Network Requests**
   - Only for IAP validation (Apple/Google)
   - No custom backend servers
   - No third-party APIs

2. **Database**
   - SQLite stored in app container
   - Encrypted by iOS/Android system
   - Sandboxed from other apps

3. **File Storage**
   - Audio files in Documents directory
   - Only accessible by VoiceFX
   - Backed up via iCloud/Google if enabled

## Cookies and Tracking

VoiceFX does not use:
- Cookies
- Web beacons
- Tracking pixels
- Session replay
- Device fingerprinting (beyond IAP validation)

## Marketing

We do not:
- Send promotional emails (no email collected)
- Display targeted ads (no ads in app)
- Share data with marketers
- Track you across apps

## Updates and Notifications

- **App Updates**: Via App Store/Play Store
- **In-App Notifications**: Local only, never pushed from server
- **Announcements**: Shown in-app only
